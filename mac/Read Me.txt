SnowDesk
========

Weather for your Mac desktop: snow, rain, sakura, leaves, fireflies and starfall.

INSTALL
  1. Drag SnowDesk into the Applications folder.
  2. Open it. A snowflake icon appears in the menu bar (there is no Dock icon).
  3. If macOS says the app is from an unidentified developer, right-click the app,
     choose Open, then Open again. You only need to do this once.

ALLOW DOCK ACCESS (recommended)
  Snow lands exactly on top of your Dock when SnowDesk can read the Dock's position.
  Open System Settings > Privacy & Security > Accessibility and switch SnowDesk on,
  or use "Allow Dock access..." in the SnowDesk menu. Without it, the Dock position
  is only estimated.

USE
  Menu-bar icon > pick an effect, wallpaper, intensity, launch at login.
  Move the pointer through snow to push it around. Leaves and petals scatter too.
  Drag a window and the snow on it slides off.
